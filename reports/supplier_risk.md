# Supplier matching and risk

Suppliers matched to the Companies House register (01 November 2026 snapshot). Generated by `spendsentinel match`.

**1,816 of 2,814 suppliers matched. They cover 90% of the spend that could be on the register** (£24.70bn of £27.58bn), and 50% of all spend once public bodies and joint ventures, which are not registered companies, are included.

`public_body` covers councils, combined authorities, NHS bodies, government departments and similar, which are not on the company register. `joint_venture` covers unincorporated joint ventures (most HS2 civil-engineering spend), which are not registered either. `not_found` had no confident match.
